# Coffee Subscriptions -- The Daily Grind

Rockabilly Roasting offers a subscription service called **The Daily Grind**. When you subscribe, your favorite coffee is roasted fresh and shipped to you on a regular schedule. You save money on every bag, and you never have to worry about running out.

---

## What Is a Coffee Subscription?

A subscription is a recurring coffee delivery. You pick a coffee, choose how often you want it delivered, and we handle the rest. Your coffee is roasted to order before each shipment -- it never sits on a shelf.

Key things to know:

- **No commitment.** You can pause, resume, or cancel anytime from your account.
- **Subscribers save money.** The discount depends on the delivery frequency you choose. More frequent deliveries save you more.
- **Always fresh.** Every bag is roasted specifically for your shipment.

---

## Browsing Subscription Plans

Visit the **Subscriptions** page (labeled "The Daily Grind" on the site) to see everything available.

### Delivery Schedules

At the top of the page, you will see the available delivery frequencies. Common options include:

- **Weekly** (every 7 days)
- **Every 2 weeks** (every 14 days)
- **Every 3 weeks** (every 21 days)
- **Monthly** (every 30 days)
- **Every 2 months** (every 60 days)
- **Every 3 months** (every 90 days)

Each schedule shows its discount -- for example, "SAVE 15%." The more frequently you order, the more you save.

### Available Coffees

Below the delivery schedules, you will see all the coffees available for subscription. Each product card shows:

- The coffee name and an image
- The roast level (light to dark)
- Tasting notes (e.g., chocolate, caramel, citrus)
- The original price with a line through it and the discounted subscription price
- A badge showing the maximum discount available

Not every product is available as a subscription. If you do not see a coffee you like on this page, it may only be available as a one-time purchase in the main catalog.

## How to Subscribe

### Step 1: Choose Your Coffee

From the Subscriptions page, click on the coffee you want. This takes you to the product detail page with the subscription options already open.

You can also start from any subscribable product in the catalog. If a product supports subscriptions, you will see a "Subscribe & Save" section on its product page.

### Step 2: Pick a Plan, Variant, and Quantity

On the product page, select:

- **Variant** -- the specific option you want (for example, a 12 oz bag or a 2 lb bag)
- **Delivery frequency** -- how often you want it shipped (e.g., every 14 days, every 30 days)
- **Quantity** -- how many bags per delivery (1 to 10)

The price updates automatically to show your discounted total. For example, if a bag normally costs $18.00 and your plan saves 10%, you will see $16.20 per bag.

### Step 3: Proceed to Subscribe

After making your selections, you are taken to the Subscribe page. This page shows a summary of your plan:

- The plan name and delivery frequency
- Your quantity and total price per delivery

### Step 4: Enter Payment Information

The subscribe page includes a secure payment form powered by Stripe. You will need to provide:

- Your email address
- Your name
- A shipping address
- A credit or debit card

Your card is saved securely so future deliveries can be charged automatically. Rockabilly Roasting does not store your card number -- Stripe handles all payment security.

### Step 5: Confirmation

Once your payment is processed, two things happen:

1. **Your subscription is created.** It starts immediately, and your first delivery is on its way.
2. **Your first order is placed.** You will receive an order confirmation email with your order details.

You will also receive a separate subscription confirmation email.

## Managing Your Subscription

After subscribing, you can manage everything from your account. Go to **Account > Subscriptions** to see all your active subscriptions.

Each subscription shows:

- The plan name and delivery frequency
- Your quantity
- The current status (Active, Paused, or Cancelled)
- Your next order date (for active subscriptions)

### Skipping a Shipment

Still have beans on the shelf, or heading out of town for a couple of weeks? Skip ahead instead of pausing -- the subscription stays active and picks its rhythm back up on its own.

1. Go to **Account > Subscriptions**.
2. Find the subscription and click **Skip a shipment**.
3. Either pick how many upcoming shipments to skip (up to 6) and click **Skip**, or pick the day you want deliveries to start again and click **Set date**. Restart dates run from the day after your next scheduled shipment up to 60 days out -- a date before that shipment would move your charge earlier, not later. If your next shipment is already more than 60 days away there is nothing to push back, so only the shipment-count option is offered.

Nothing is charged for a skipped shipment -- you only pay for coffee we actually send. Your next order date updates right away, and the normal cadence continues from there. Need a longer break than 60 days? Pause instead.

We email you as soon as a skip goes through -- whether you did it yourself or asked the shop to -- confirming what was skipped and when the next shipment bills.

### Undoing a Skip

Skipped by mistake? The confirmation email has an **Undo this skip** link. It opens a page showing the date the skip moved you to and the date undoing puts you back to; nothing changes until you press the button. The same **Undo skip** button appears on the subscription card in **Account > Subscriptions**.

Undo restores the exact schedule you had before, and is available until either that original shipment date passes or the schedule moves for another reason (a renewal, a resume, a plan change, or a second skip). After that, reply to the email and we will sort the dates out with you.

### Pausing a Subscription

If you need a break -- maybe you are going on vacation or you have extra coffee stacked up -- you can pause your subscription.

1. Go to **Account > Subscriptions**.
2. Find the subscription you want to pause.
3. Click **Pause**.
4. Confirm by clicking **Yes, pause**.

While paused, no orders will be placed and no charges will occur. Your subscription will stay paused until you resume it.

### Resuming a Subscription

Ready for more coffee? Resume your subscription at any time.

1. Go to **Account > Subscriptions**.
2. Find the paused subscription.
3. Click **Resume**, then confirm.

The paused card and the confirmation both name the date your next order goes out, and you get an email saying the same thing.

You are not made to wait out a whole cycle. Resuming puts your next order on the **next roasting run** -- always within a day -- and your usual rhythm picks up from there. So a 30-day subscriber who resumes on March 15 ships on the 16th and is next up around April 15, not on April 14 with nothing in between.

### Cancelling a Subscription

You can cancel at any time with no penalty.

1. Go to **Account > Subscriptions**.
2. Find the subscription you want to cancel.
3. Click **Cancel**.
4. Confirm by clicking **Yes, cancel**.

After cancellation, no further orders or charges will occur. The cancellation date is recorded on your account. Cancelled subscriptions cannot be restarted -- if you change your mind later, you can set up a new subscription.

## Billing and Delivery

### How Billing Works

- **First order:** You are charged when you subscribe. This covers your first delivery.
- **Recurring charges:** Your card is charged automatically at the start of each new delivery cycle. The charge matches your subscription price (variant price minus your plan discount, times your quantity).
- **Payment method:** Your card is saved on file with Stripe during your initial subscription. Future charges are processed automatically.

### How the Renewal Cycle Works

Your subscription runs on a fixed day cycle based on the plan you chose. For example:

- **Every 14 Days plan:** A new order is placed every 14 days.
- **Every 30 Days plan:** A new order is placed every 30 days.

The "Next order" date shown on your subscription page tells you exactly when your next delivery will be charged and shipped.

### What Happens if a Payment Fails

If your card cannot be charged on the renewal date, your subscription is marked as **Past Due**. The system will retry the charge. If retries continue to fail, the subscription may be cancelled automatically. To avoid this, make sure your card on file is up to date.

### Multiple Subscriptions

You can subscribe to multiple coffees. Each subscription is independent -- you can have one coffee on a 14-day schedule and another on a 30-day schedule. Each subscription renews as its own separate order, even if multiple subscriptions come due on the same day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change which coffee I receive?**
Product swaps are not yet available through self-service. To change your coffee, cancel your current subscription and start a new one with the coffee you prefer.

**Can I change my delivery frequency?**
Frequency changes are not yet available through self-service. Cancel and re-subscribe with your preferred schedule.

**Can I skip just one delivery?**
Skipping a single delivery is not yet supported. You can pause your subscription and resume it when you are ready for the next delivery.

**Do I need an account to subscribe?**
An account is created for you automatically when you subscribe (using the email address you provide during checkout). You can sign in anytime using a magic link sent to that email. See the [Account Guide](account.md) for details.

**Is there a minimum commitment?**
No. You can cancel after your very first delivery if you want.
