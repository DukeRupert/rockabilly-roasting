# Applying for a Wholesale Account

## What Is a Wholesale Account?

A wholesale account gives your business access to volume pricing and a dedicated ordering portal at Rockabilly Roasting. Wholesale accounts are designed for:

- Cafes and coffee shops
- Restaurants and bars
- Offices and co-working spaces
- Retail shops reselling packaged coffee
- Brands interested in white-label roasting (your label, our beans)

With a wholesale account you get bulk pricing on all products, the ability to place orders on invoice (no payment collected at the time of order), and a streamlined quick-order interface built for repeat purchasing.

## Finding the Application Page

From any page on the Rockabilly Roasting website, navigate to the **Wholesale & White Label** page. You can find it in the site navigation or go directly to:

```
https://rockabillyroasting.com/wholesale
```

This page explains the two programs available -- wholesale purchasing and white-label roasting -- and includes an **Apply Now** button that takes you to the application form.

If you already have an account, there is a **Sign in here** link at the bottom of the page that takes you to the wholesale login instead.

## What You Need to Apply

The application form is at `/wholesale/apply`. You will need to provide:

**Required fields:**

- **First name** -- your first name as the primary contact
- **Last name** -- your last name
- **Email address** -- this becomes your login email, so use an address you check regularly
- **Company name** -- the legal or operating name of your business

**Optional fields:**

- **Phone number** -- a contact number where the team can reach you
- **Website** -- your business website (helps the team understand your operation)

Fill in all required fields and click **Submit Application**.

## What Happens After You Submit

After submitting, you will see a confirmation screen with the message:

> "Thank you for your interest! We'll review your application and get back to you within 2 business days."

Here is what happens behind the scenes:

1. Your application is recorded in the system with a status of **pending**.
2. The Rockabilly Roasting team is notified of the new application.
3. A staff member reviews your information -- they may look at your business website, location, and the type of account you would need.
4. The team either **approves** or **declines** the application.

## If You Are Approved

When your application is approved, you will receive an email with a link to set up your account password. The link takes you to a setup page where you create your login credentials. See the [Getting Started](getting-started.md) guide for details on that process.

## If You Are Declined

If your application is not approved, the team may reach out to you directly with more information. Declined applications include internal notes for the staff but you will not receive an automated rejection notice through the system. If you have not heard back within a few business days, contact the team at the shop (509-585-2320) or through the website contact form.

## Already Applied?

If you have already applied and been approved, do not submit a second application -- the system will reject duplicate email addresses. Instead, use the **Sign in** link on the application page or go directly to:

```
https://rockabillyroasting.com/wholesale/login
```

If you applied but never received a setup link, contact the team to check on your application status.
